## Changes made to the analysis after the first check (2026-11-02) — numbers in the text that must change

Two methodological changes were made after the check below (see README, *Methods in brief*):

1. **Intervals are now 95 % prediction intervals** (observed − `up`, observed − `lp`) instead of the
   95 % CI of the baseline. Every interval in Figures 1 and 3, Table A2 and the text changes; point
   estimates do not. The PIs are wider, so fewer cells are "significant".
2. **Regional baselines are fitted on the regional totals** instead of summing state bounds. Regional
   point estimates move by < 0.1 per 100,000; the intervals are now coherent with the state and US ones.

## Methods text vs. what the code does

| Methods says | Code does |
|---|---|
| "Generalized Additive Model" | quasi-Poisson **GLM**, log-linear in year, log-population offset |
